## Edits actually performed

- EXIF auto-orientation, including the 90-degree orientation metadata in 4.jpg. No perspective distortion or arbitrary straightening of intentional diagonal compositions.
- Manual rectangular crops to remove Instagram arrows, profile/account overlays, pagination/reply controls, black edges, and surplus wall space. Packaging labels and text physically on desserts are retained as authentic product details.
- A **3.5% brightness increase on 5.jpg** and **2.5% on 9.jpg**. All other exposures were left unchanged. No hue, saturation, white-balance, or selective contrast adjustment was applied; the strong warm lighting in 20.jpg remains visible.
- Restrained output sharpening on selected derivatives: Sharp `sigma: 0.4, m1: 0.35, m2: 0.7`. This is not AI detail recovery.
- Resize to **at most 1400px wide, without enlargement**, WebP quality 86 / effort 6, metadata removed by the export, and a tiny real-image blur placeholder. Cookie image remains 1000px wide; screenshots were not upscaled.
- Selected sources total **10.87 MB**, versus **3.73 MB** for the 18 WebP derivatives (about **66% smaller**), excluding the separate social JPEG. Responsive Next.js Image adds device-size WebP/AVIF delivery and lazy loading; only the main hero is prioritized on Home.

The original screenshots still carry their original artifacts on disk by design; they are not the files delivered by page image components. Crop selections were reviewed visually at contact-sheet and page scale, including a second pass that removed the small profile marks in 12/13.

## Updating or reproducing

```bash
npm ci
npm run photos:optimize
```

- `scripts/photo-recipes.mjs`: source number, exact crop rectangle `[left, top, width, height]` in **auto-oriented source pixels**, optional brightness, title, and factual alt text.
- `scripts/optimize-photos.mjs`: deterministic export pipeline; never writes the originals. Requires the explicit Sharp development dependency.
- `src/lib/photo-manifest.json`: output paths, dimensions, bytes, source SHA-256 checksums, and blur placeholders. Generated; do not hand-edit.
- `src/lib/photography.ts`: editable placements for hero, cards, story, inquiry, Contact, and Instagram.
- `src/lib/site.ts`: gallery order/categories and unchanged menu/pricing source of truth.
